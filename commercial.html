Commercial Day Old Chicks

Our high-quality Commercial Day-Old Chicks (DOCs) are available for purchase and come with technical support to ensure optimal performance at your farm. They are produced from our Parent Stock and are also raised under strict hygiene, biosecurity, and optimal hatchery conditions. These DOCs are ideal for farmers focused on meat or egg production and benefit from Justran Farms’ integrated breeding system, ensuring superior genetics, consistency, and reliable supply.

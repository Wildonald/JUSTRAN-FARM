OUR STORY

Rooted in nature,
driven by science.

Justran Farms Ltd. is an integrated poultry operation with breeding, commercial broiler processing, aquaculture, and veterinary services — all under one roof.

"From hatchery to harvest — we power every link in Nigeria's poultry value chain."

— justran Farms, Est. 2004

We provide parent stock to hatcheries, distribute commercial chicks, and support customers with post- sales technical services. Our aquaculture division breeds premium catfish while our veterinary division supplies high-quality products and diagnostics.

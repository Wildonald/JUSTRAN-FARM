Poultry

JUSTRAN Farms operates Grand Parent Stock (GPS) farms that produce Parent Stock (PS) for both broiler and layer lines. Our broiler genetics are Arbor Acres from Aviagen, while our layer genetics are ISA Brown from Hendrix Genetics. By controlling the breeding chain from GPS to PS and commercial Day Old Chicks, CHINYERE Farms delivers reliable supply, strong genetic performance, and consistent quality across Nigeria's poultry value chain.

Parent Stock Day Old Chicks

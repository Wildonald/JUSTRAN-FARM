Bio-Security

Biosecurity at Justran Farms goes beyond disease response — it is a structured, preventive system built into every level of our operations. We enforce strict access controls, sanitation routines, disinfection procedures, and hygiene protocols across all our facilities. We also supply and recommend the disinfection chemicals, sanitation products, and biosecurity equipment used to uphold these standards — making CHI Farms a complete resource for farmers building or strengthening their own biosecurity programs.
